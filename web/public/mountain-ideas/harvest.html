Harvesting: the field asks, the tool answers

Two ways that work together, drawn over real screenshots of the game. A field that is ready shows a sickle token over it; one click sends the farmers to the whole field. For anything finer, a harvest tool in the build card marks crops by dragging, like soil or paths.

1. The field asks: a sickle token over every ripe field

When a field is ripe, a small paper token with a sickle rises over its middle and bobs gently; the number is how many tiles are ready. No menu: one click and the farmers come.

2. The tool answers: harvest in the build card

A sickle tool beside erase. Drag across crops: only the ripe tiles are marked (gold), green ones are left; a click marks one tile. Marks show as holograms like everything else in the card, so they can be taken back before the farmers set out.
