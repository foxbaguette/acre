Tiles

Field preview

A random island farm built from the tiles below (every island gets its own coastline of peninsulas, bays and islets), with crops growing on the soil. "Natural edges" rounds and softens where soil, path and water meet the grass, worked out in code from the same square tiles. "Animation" adds small, cheap touches of life: glinting water, crops swaying in wind gusts, drifting cloud shadows and a few butterflies. "Creatures" lets the favourites live here: land creatures in the fenced paddocks and on open grass, sea creatures in the ponds, flyers in the sky. Pick the weather below the toggles. Scroll, pinch or double-click to zoom, drag to look around, Shuffle for another farm.

Grass

20 variants. Most are plain grass, some have a tuft, stone, clover, flowers, a twig or a leaf.

Fences

Transparent overlays for grass tiles. Put fence-NESW on a tile, where each digit is 1 when the neighbour on that side (north, east, south, west) also has a fence. These 16 tiles build any enclosure.

Bridges

Transparent overlays for water tiles where a path crosses. bridge-h runs east-west and bridge-v north-south; a digit is 1 when the bridge continues on that side and 0 at the bank, where it gets its posts.

Soil, path and water

Beach sand, open sea, prepared soil for planting, 4 cobblestone path variants, and 6 pond water variants.
