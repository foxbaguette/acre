Field preview

Islands saved on the WAX blockchain (contract unclaimedlnd). Pick one under Map: its ground, mountains, water and forests come straight from the tiles stored on the chain, and the small details, grass colour and life are added here, the same way on every visit. The last entry, Random island, builds a random farm here instead, with crops growing on the soil. "Natural edges" rounds and softens where soil, path and water meet the grass, worked out in code from the same square tiles. "Animation" adds small, cheap touches of life: glinting water, crops swaying in wind gusts, drifting cloud shadows and a few butterflies. "Creatures" lets the favourites live here: land creatures in the fenced paddocks and on open grass, sea creatures in the ponds, flyers in the sky. Pick the weather below the toggles. Scroll, pinch or double-click to zoom, drag to look around, Shuffle for another random farm.

Island generator settings

Grass

20 variants. Most are plain grass, some have a tuft, stone, clover, flowers, a twig or a leaf.

Fences

Transparent overlays for grass tiles. Put fence-NESW on a tile, where each digit is 1 when the neighbour on that side (north, east, south, west) also has a fence. These 16 tiles build any enclosure.

Bridges

Transparent overlays for water tiles where a path crosses. bridge-h runs east-west and bridge-v north-south; a digit is 1 when the bridge continues on that side and 0 at the bank, where it gets its posts.

Soil, path and water

Beach sand, open sea, prepared soil for planting, 4 cobblestone path variants, and 6 pond water variants.
