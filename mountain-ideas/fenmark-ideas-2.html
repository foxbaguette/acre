The Fenmark (FEN), round two: in the spirit of 5, 7, 11 and 13

The four chosen from round one come first (5, 7, 11, 13), then the new ideas, A to P. What those four share: one clear emblem struck in relief on plain metal, mostly silver; nature, not the fox's face; the calm of the journal. Each new idea large in silver, and small in silver and in gold, on a light and a dark wallet.
