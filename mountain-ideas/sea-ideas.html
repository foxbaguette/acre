Wrecks and reefs, seen through the water

Ideas for the sea floor. Everything is shown as the game would show it: on sand, under water that takes the colour out and tints it blue-green with depth, with light rippling over it.

Shipwrecks

Ten wrecks, drawn from their real build: planking, ribs, keel, silt drifted over them, growth on them.

Reefs

Ten kinds of reef. Each is a way of growing, not a fixed drawing: it fills whatever shape it is given. Left a small patch, right a large stretch.
