Chapter I

A chart of your own island

Every island is generated for you and owned by your wallet. No two are alike, so every chart in this journal is a first edition.

What we know so far

  • Every island is generated from scratch, never copied.
  • It is owned by your wallet: the record is yours, not a server’s.
  • No two islands are alike. Coastlines, hills and forests all differ.

Landforms spotted so far:

  • Peninsulas
  • Bays
  • Beaches
  • Forests
  • Snowy mountains
  • Lakes
