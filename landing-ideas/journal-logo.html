Logo options for the field journal

Four journal-style logos built around one pen sketch of an island. Each is shown large and as it would sit in the page header.

AThe field station stamp

Unclaimed Acres The Island · Creatures · Crops

A worn rubber stamp in rust ink, like the mark of a remote field station. Bold and recognisable; the ring works on its own as an app icon or favicon.

BThe bookplate

The Island · Creatures · Crops

An engraved bookplate glued inside the cover: "ex libris, vol. I". Elegant and very bookish; the label shape can frame other UI too (panels, item names).

CThe inked title page

Unclaimed Acres The Island · Creatures · Crops

The title page of a journal: the island drawn in blue ink above an italic title with a hand-drawn flourish. The quietest and most literary option.

DThe notebook cover

The Island · Creatures · Crops

A paper label on a moss-green cloth notebook cover, like a real expedition notebook. Strong as a game icon and on the wallet login screen; a bit less playful.
