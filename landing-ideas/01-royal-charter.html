An island, granted to you alone.

Unclaimed Acres is a calm island farming and creature game on the WAX blockchain. Sow sixty-four crops, keep more than fifty creatures, and tend your own shores at whatever pace pleases you.

Read the charter

Part the First

The Land

Be it known that each island is a thing apart: raised from the sea once, written down for good, and held in your name.

  1. I

    Of its Making

    Every island is generated and stored on the WAX blockchain. Its coasts, hills and woods are recorded there, not merely painted.

  2. II

    Of its Keeping

    The island is owned by your wallet. The deed is yours to hold.

  3. III

    Of its Character

    No two islands are alike. Yours may hold peninsulas and sheltered bays, long beaches, deep forests, quiet lakes, and terraced mountains capped with snow.

  4. IV

    Of its Hours

    No hurry is written into this charter. Play at your own pace; the land will keep.

Part the Second

The Residents

More than fifty creatures, from the humble fox to the kirin and the phoenix, each drawn twice: once in profile, as hung here, and once from above, as they wander your island.
