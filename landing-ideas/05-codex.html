Side views fill the pages of your codex; the top-down sprite is how the same creature appears on your island map, wandering the forests, beaches and bays. Same faceted, outline-free style, from fox to phoenix.

Eight stages, seed to harvest

Every crop is drawn through eight stages: the seed, its sowing, three rounds of growth, the harvest in the field, the produce in your hands, and the wilted plant if you wander off for too long.
